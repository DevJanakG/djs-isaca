# S01 — night highway

The emotional purpose is departure into an uncertain investigation: quiet, grounded and isolated. The black classic car is the single hero element.

The camera sits approximately 1.25 m above the road, behind and to the left of the car, with a 38° vertical FOV. Its target places the road's vanishing point left of center. A restrained forward dolly precedes a small lateral move between local progress 0.60 and 0.85; there is no pointer steering or arbitrary wobble.

Depth comes from close woodland occlusion, near shoulder vegetation, mid-distance woodland, and exponential fog with three low-opacity mist layers. One soft directional moonlight motivates cool rim lighting and the local sky reflection map. Minimal sky fill and restrained rear-light warmth preserve black bodywork and dark forest areas.

The car remains fixed in the shot; road texture, worn paint and vegetation share eased world travel. The sky, mist layers and editorial text position remain still. Presenter text reveals at 0.30–0.55, the title at 0.45–0.75, supporting copy at 0.60–0.85, and all copy fades at 0.85–1.00.

## Scope and performance

S01 uses the existing persistent canvas, global progress and scene range. Only its visual implementation and the S01 fog/shadow configuration in `World.tsx` are replaced. Vegetation uses three instanced draws, paint uses one, shadows use a single 1024 map, DPR remains capped, and no post-processing passes are added. Source model geometry is unchanged; a cloned embedded ground quad is removed at runtime.

## Visual QA

Browser review covered 1920×1080, 1440×900, 390×844, and reduced motion at 1440×900. Screenshots captured local progress 0.02, 0.28, 0.55, 0.75 and 0.97, plus the S02 handoff. The actual eased wheel scroll, case-file link, journal return to S01, and return to S00 were exercised. All four paths passed the timing, navigation, overflow and runtime-error checks. Screenshot evidence and machine findings are in `/tmp/s01-qa-*` and `/tmp/s01-visual-qa.json` for this session.

Review fixes included the supplied model's embedded platform, its initially invisible physical material, road-paint sorting, foliage card edge clipping, shoulder vegetation obstructing the foreground, mobile vehicle cropping, and UI overlap with the journal/HUD. The rendered S01 frames contain textured surfaces and foliage rather than the rejected untextured car/tree placeholders.

`npm run build` and `npm run lint` pass. The existing shared WebGL bundle still produces Vite's size warning; desktop R3F produces a Three.Clock deprecation warning. Screenshot readbacks can produce GPU-stall warnings in software Chromium. No console errors, page errors or failed asset requests were recorded. This review is not a hardware frame-rate benchmark.
